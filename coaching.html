Coach John Gagiu

Real coaching from
someone who has

John Gagiu holds a Master's degree in Psychology, is a Gottman Level 1 certified practitioner and spent over a decade as a church minister in Newcastle — roles that required him to sit with people at their most vulnerable.

What makes his coaching different is not his credentials. It is that he has navigated the same emotional terrain his clients face — divorce, attachment wounds, the long work of rebuilding. He is not reading from a textbook. He has lived the chapters.

Most people do not need more advice. They need someone who can see what they cannot see yet.
