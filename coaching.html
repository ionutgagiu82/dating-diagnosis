About Coach John

I've sat with people
in their worst moments.
This is what I've learned.

From 2004 to 2016, I served as a pastor and associate pastor — counselling hundreds of families through relationship breakdown, disconnection, and loss. At the same time, I worked as a human rights activist. I spent over a decade studying how people hurt each other, and how they heal.

Then my own marriage ended in 2020. And everything I thought I knew about connection was tested at the deepest level. I went back to the books — this time as a student. I completed a Master's degree in Psychology. I trained at the Gottman Institute, one of the world's leading research centres for relationships.

I'm not a therapist. I'm not here to fix your childhood. I'm here to tell you what's actually happening in your dating life — with the honesty of someone who has studied it, lived it, and helped hundreds of people through it.

What we work on

The things nobody
else will say out loud.

01
Your Pattern

We identify your specific dating blocker — not a general problem, but the precise dynamic that keeps showing up. Then we build a plan to change it.

02
Your Situations

Got a date coming up? Not sure how to respond to a message? Need to have a difficult conversation? I help you handle the specific situations in front of you — with clarity and confidence.

03
Your Social Life

Most people who struggle with dating have a deeper problem: they don't socialise enough. We fix that — building real-world habits that put you in situations where connection is possible.

04
Your Courage

Approaching. Initiating. Expressing interest. Saying the thing. These feel impossible until you've done them enough times. I hold you accountable to doing them.

05
Your Beliefs

Half the battle is in your head — the stories you tell yourself about what you deserve, what's possible, what other people think. We replace the limiting ones with something grounded and real.

06
Your Momentum

Most people know what they should do. The problem is consistency. Weekly support keeps you moving — accountable to the actions you've committed to, not just the intentions you've had.
