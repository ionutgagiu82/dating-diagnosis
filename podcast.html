Your Host

Coach John Gagiu

John Gagiu is a dating coach, former church minister and creator of The Connection Code — a relationship improvement ecosystem based in Newcastle upon Tyne. He holds a Master's degree in Psychology and is a Gottman Level 1 certified practitioner.

The podcast is his way of bringing that knowledge to anyone who needs it — honest, direct and without the usual platitudes. No scripts. No guest panels for the sake of it. Just real conversations about the things that matter most.
