The Four Attachment Styles

Which one is you?

S

Secure

Comfortable with closeness and independence. Can give and receive love without constant anxiety or avoidance.

A

Anxious

Craves closeness but fears abandonment. Often overthinks messages, seeks reassurance, reads too deeply into everything.

Av

Avoidant

Values independence above all. Pulls back when things get close. Struggles with vulnerability and being truly known.

FA

Fearful Avoidant

Wants deep connection but also fears it. Caught between longing for closeness and the instinct to protect themselves from it.

Why This Matters

Your attachment style shapes every relationship you have ever had

Most people have never heard the term attachment style — yet it quietly governs who they are attracted to, how they behave when things get close, and why the same painful patterns keep appearing.

Understanding yours does not fix everything overnight. But it changes the way you see yourself — and that changes everything else.
